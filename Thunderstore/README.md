# EnemyCritFix

Affects the following enemies:
* Beetle
* Jellyfish
* Beetle Guard (slam attack)
* Bighorn Bison
* Lynx Berserker (slam attack)
* Stone Golem
* Parent
* Clay Apothecary (melee part of the slam)
* Halcyonite (laser & whirlwind)
* Lynx Totem (slam attack & spire direct hit)
* Magma/Overloading Worm (contact damage)
* Xi Construct (laser finisher)
* Solus Amalgamator (shock pulse & energy orbs)
    * Also fixes the shock pluse inflicting ownerless damage.
* Severed Breaker
* Shruum (contact damage & landing attack)
* Mithrix (hammer slam)

As a treat, I've included a config that grants a bonus crit chance to all enemies. It's disabled by default, but if you want to have some fun...
